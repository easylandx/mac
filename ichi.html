The Japanese Ichimatsu 市松 pattern is a simple alternating checkerboard of squares. Its continuous, repeating geometry is traditionally associated with prosperity, growth, longevity, and an enduring continuation into the future. The name Ichimatsu comes from the Edo-period kabuki actor Sanogawa Ichimatsu, who popularized the checkered design. Because the pattern can continue endlessly without interruption, it is also regarded as an auspicious symbol of continuity and good fortune.
