Ali Baba & The Forty Thieves

A timeless tale of secret riches, treacherous bandits, and the unmatched cleverness of a courageous heroine.

The Classic Tale

Ali Baba, a humble woodcutter in Persia, unexpectedly discovers a secret cave guarded by a ruthless band of forty thieves. By overhearing their mystical incantation—"Open Sesame"—he unlocks immensurable wealth hidden inside the mountain.

However, when his greedy brother Cassim discovers the secret, tragedy strikes. What follows is a high-stakes duel of wits between the vengeful Thief Captain and Morgiana, Ali Baba's clever slave girl, whose brilliance saves the family from certain destruction.

The ruthless leader of the 40 thieves. Relentless in his quest for revenge, he hatches elaborate schemes—including disguise in oil jars—to destroy Ali Baba.

Iconic Elements

Core symbols that define the legend

"Open Sesame"

The world-famous incantation that commands the solid rock face to open, symbolizing secret knowledge and fortune.

The 38 Oil Jars

A cunning Trojan horse plot where thieves hide inside oil containers, foiled by Morgiana’s swift boiling oil defense.

The Hidden Hoard

Mountains of gold pieces, silk tapestries, and glittering jewels accumulated over decades of highway robbery.

Interactive Story Timeline

Follow the chronological journey from discovery to victory

Act I

Discovery in the Forest

While gathering firewood, Ali Baba hides from approaching riders and overhears the magic words opening the secret cavern.

Act II

Cassim's Fatal Mistake

Cassim forces the secret from Ali Baba. He enters the cave, fills his bags with gold, but forgets the password ("Open Barley? Open Wheat?") and is trapped inside.

Act III

The Oil Jar Plot

The Thieves track Ali Baba down. The Captain brings 38 oil jars to Ali Baba's yard—37 housing hidden bandits. Morgiana discovers the plot and neutralizes them.

Act IV

The Dagger Dance

The Captain returns disguised as a merchant. Performing an intricate sword dance for the guests, Morgiana strikes down the Captain, securing freedom and peace forever.
