Maya Glyph Simulator

Classic Maya writing mixed two kinds of signs freely: logograms, whole pictures for whole words, and syllabograms, phonetic signs spelling out consonant-vowel sounds. A scribe could carve a word as one logogram, spell it out entirely in syllables, or blend both in the same glyph block — and this stone does the same, one strike at a time.
