01

The straight line that wasn't

She used to believe growth worked like a formula: set a goal, work hard, arrive. But life doesn't move that way — and neither does she. It's messier than the plan ever allowed for.

In-between

The space between who you were and who you're becoming

Some days she feels driven, certain, like she could do anything. Other days she doesn't recognize herself — everything that made sense a few months ago suddenly doesn't. She calls this stretch the "in-between spaces": not quite the old self, not yet the new one.

She's been repeating affirmations, hoping her brain will eventually rewire itself and old patterns will fall away. But patterns don't let go that easily.

02

The dull, sluggish nothing

The feeling that nothing is happening — like life is paused and she's lost the button to press play. Her heart is screaming to get out of the rut, and still she can't seem to make the change stick. Shame creeps in: for not being consistent, for having dreams she wants but isn't moving toward.

On the hard nights, she cries and wonders if she's the problem. Maybe she doesn't have what it takes. Maybe she's weak, lazy, ungrateful — words she never wanted to use on herself. This is the part of her she's been trying to outgrow, the reason she keeps moving from city to city, looking for a fix, a purpose, someone to hand her a map.

No one will come
and save you.

Nothing disappears just because you move

The doubts and fears don't vanish overnight just because the city changes. She may find people to walk alongside her, but no one can walk the path for her. There is no shortcut to becoming herself.

And the irony is, she already knew where the answer was. She'd just been searching for it in other people, new places, and better versions of herself she hadn't become yet — when it was always here, in the doing.

03

The quiet, unglamorous choices

Not a breakthrough. Not waiting for everything to click. Just the small, ordinary decisions made when no one is watching:

  • Getting out of the house and exercising, even when she feels sad

  • Reaching out to people instead of isolating herself

  • Picking up the camera and hitting record before she feels ready

Small wins. They don't look like much from the outside. But growth isn't a finish line — it's the way she moves through the mess, the way she talks to herself when she stumbles, and the way she keeps reaching and fighting when it would be easier to give up.

Maybe becoming isn't about being more.
Maybe it's about being here.
