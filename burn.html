Write it.
Burn it.

A small ritual for a restless mind. Put your intention on paper, then let the fire make it real.

Begin the ritual

Write what you want on a blank sheet of paper and then burn it.

The brain likes rituals.

It doesn't matter if they are spiritual or not.

What matters is that you truly believe in them.

Fire makes the intention feel real. And what feels real can activate the subconscious.

Your blank sheet

Write one clear intention. Read it once. Then release it.

How to make it count

1

Choose one intention. Write it in the present, as if it were already true.

2

Breathe slowly and read it aloud. Mean every word.

3

Burn it safely, over a fireproof dish, away from anything flammable.

4

Watch it vanish, then let it go and act on it tomorrow.

Rituals do not replace action, they start it. The burn above is virtual. If you do it for real, keep water close.
