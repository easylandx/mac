Notes & Curiosities

Lesser‑Known Facts

  • ♪ Ice cream truck theme. The tune is known worldwide today largely because it's one of the most common pre‑programmed melodies used by modern ice cream trucks.
  • ♫ A destroyed opera. After a manager absconded with his box‑office funds, Joplin couldn't cover his rent — and his boarding‑house landlord confiscated and destroyed the score of his first full opera.
  • ♪ A prophetic prediction. Stung by critics who dismissed his music as a cheap fad, Joplin predicted he'd only be appreciated long after he was gone — and he was right.
  • ♫ An unmarked grave. Joplin died destitute and was buried in a pauper's grave with no headstone. He finally got one in 1974 — the same year his music won an Oscar.
  • ♪ The "penny" contract. His first hit, "Maple Leaf Rag," earned him just a penny in royalties per copy sold — but as sales exploded, that penny added up to a steady income.
  • ♫ Free lessons. Joplin's career was launched by a German immigrant music teacher who spotted his talent and taught him classical theory free of charge.
