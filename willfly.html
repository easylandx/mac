The Airport Test

Does this page actually serve the business you're trying to build?

Before touching design or copy, we ask a simple question: five years from now, does this page — and the customers it brings in — support the business you actually want to be running? If it doesn't fit that picture, it's not worth building well.

Skip ThisBuilding on assumption

A page built around what you assume customers want, launched without ever checking, is a bet made with real time and real budget — and it's the single biggest reason small-business pages go live and go quiet.

Do ThisBuilding on evidence

A page tested against real buying intent before the full build means every dollar spent on design and copy goes toward something already shown to convert — not a guess dressed up nicely.

The Instrument Panel

Every test ends with a clear reading

No guessing games. Once real customers respond to the test page, the data points to exactly one of three moves.

Green light

Bookings or pre-orders hit the target. We move straight into the full build.

Caution

Interest is there but mixed. We adjust the offer or audience and test again.

Hold

No real buying intent. We stop here — before a single extra hour or dollar is spent.
