Field Rules

Three rules for a landing page that earns its keep

No unnecessary complexity. Every rule below exists to move a visitor toward one decision: contact this business.

RULE 01

One job per page

A landing page isn't a brand site. It exists to answer one question — "can this business solve my problem?" — and end with one clear action. Everything else is noise.

RULE 02

Local trust, built in

Real photos, a real phone number, a real service area up top. Global competitors can't fake local proximity — your page shouldn't hide it either.

RULE 03

Fast beats fancy

A page that loads in under two seconds on a work truck's phone signal outperforms a beautiful page that doesn't. Speed is a conversion feature, not a technical detail.
