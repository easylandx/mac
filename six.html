Terms — non-negotiable

Six things
success demands

Not talent. Not luck. These. Read them like terms you're agreeing to — because you are.

SCROLL
01
Average, repeated, becomes excellence

Consistency

Consistency is what turns average into excellence. Not one great day — the same effort, shown up for, again and again. Without it, greater success isn't just harder to reach. It's impossible.

02
No shortcut exists

Hard work

Reject the idea of luck. Reject the shortcut you're waiting for. There isn't one — there never was. The only route through the work is more work.

03
Slow, then sudden

Patience

Progress is slow at first, then sudden. Most people quit right before the turn. The moment you lose patience, you lose the battle — not to a rival, but to yourself.

04
Pay it now, or pay it later

Sacrifice

Everything you want carries a price. If you won't pay it, the thing you wanted becomes the sacrifice instead. One or the other is always given up — you choose which.

05
After the motivation fades

Discipline

Motivation gets you started. Discipline is what shows up after it leaves — especially on the days you don't feel like it. That day is the only one that actually counts.

06
Their opinion isn't the compass

Self‑confidence

Be okay with being disliked, and let your goals be disliked too. Other people's opinions were never meant to set your direction — only yours was.

End of terms

Six terms. No exceptions.

None of these are optional, and none of them are talent. They're decisions, repeated until they look like talent to everyone watching from outside.
