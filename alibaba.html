Ali Baba
& the Forty Thieves

In a land of ancient forests and bustling markets, a poor woodcutter discovers a secret that could change his life forever.

CHAPTER 01

The Hidden Cave

One day, Ali Baba was gathering wood in the forest when he saw a band of riders approach a rocky hillside. Their leader stood before a great stone and called, “Open Sesame!” The rock slid aside, revealing a cavern filled with glittering treasure. When the thieves departed, Ali Baba spoke the same words. The stone opened, and he stepped inside.

CHAPTER 02

A Dangerous Secret

Ali Baba took only a modest share of the riches and returned home. But secrets can travel quickly. His brother, Cassim, grew consumed by greed and went to the cave himself. Inside, he became so overwhelmed by the treasure that he forgot the words needed to escape. The thieves soon returned, and the danger grew.

CHAPTER 03

Courage and Cleverness

The thieves set out to discover who had found their hiding place. Yet Ali Baba was not alone: his household servant Morgiana was sharp-eyed, brave, and resourceful. Through quick thinking and fearless action, she protected the household and outwitted the thieves’ schemes.

CHAPTER 04

What the Treasure Taught

In the end, Ali Baba’s fortune was secured not by gold alone, but by the loyalty and intelligence of those around him. The tale became a reminder that greed can cloud judgment, while courage, gratitude, and quick thinking can turn danger into deliverance.
